Scan Safe uses the Open Food Facts database under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0), individual contents under the Database Contents License (DbCL 1.0), and product photographs under CC BY-SA. Preserve the attribution and applicable share-alike requirements when redistributing data. This package does not claim ownership of retailer, manufacturer or product photographs and text.

- Terms and attribution: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use
- ODbL: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
- DbCL: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/
- Additive taxonomy source: https://static.openfoodfacts.org/data/taxonomies/additives.json

The product catalogue keeps its observation dates, expiration dates, source URLs and licence fields in the source files under `stuff/`. Retailer records retain their exact product page links. The app displays product sources in its result screen.

Expo, React, React Native and other dependencies keep their own licences in the packages installed by npm. They are external dependencies and have not been copied into the app source. No proprietary Yuka code, database, graphics or rating engine is included.
